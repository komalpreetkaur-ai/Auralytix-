CRITICAL

Direct answer near the top of the page

medium effort · 1 page

AI answers quote self-contained passages. A page that opens with positioning copy and defers the substance gives nothing quotable, so it gets skipped in favour of a page that answers immediately.

NOW
lead paragraph is only 5 words
SHOULD BE
One H1, then a ~50-word paragraph that answers the page's question outright.
HOW
Put a direct, self-contained answer of roughly 50 words immediately after the H1, before any preamble.
EXAMPLE
<h1>Bajaj Bikes & Motorcycles Price in Akurdi, pune</h1>
<p>Bajaj Bikes & Motorcycles Price in Akurdi, pune is a ... that helps ... to ... . It ... , and is used by ... .</p>

HIGH

Page declares a meaningful entity type

medium effort · 1 page

SiteNavigationElement and WebPage describe the page's furniture. An AI system needs to know what the page is ABOUT -- the organisation, the article, the product.

NOW
only structural types: GeoCoordinates, MotorcycleDealer, PostalAddress
SHOULD BE
@type names what the page is: Organization, Product, Service, Article or FAQPage.
HOW
Declare the page's substantive entity: Organization or LocalBusiness for the site, Article for editorial, Product/Service for offerings, FAQPage for Q&A.
EXAMPLE
<script type="application/ld+json">
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "Organization",
  "name": "Your company",
  "url": "https://shourya-bajaj-akurdi-pune.com/",
  "logo": "https://shourya-bajaj-akurdi-pune.com/logo.png",
  "sameAs": ["https://www.linkedin.com/company/..."]
}
</script>

HIGH

medium effort · 1 page

NOW

SHOULD BE

HOW

EXAMPLE

HOW
